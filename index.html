Droplet — Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

Droplet is a game for Android made by occupyjoshua. This policy explains what the app does with your information: nothing leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Droplet has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It asks for no Android permissions and never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress, best scores and a short log of recent runs in its own private storage on your phone. Nobody else can read these files, including us, and they are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them.

Children

Droplet collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
