Droplet — Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

Droplet is a game for Android made by occupyjoshua. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Droplet has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and the game's own code never connects to the internet.

The optional purchase

One optional one-time purchase unlocks more chapters. Google Play handles it through the Google Play Billing Library, which is built into the app. That library adds the Android permissions Droplet lists: billing, internet access, and network state. Google uses them to process the purchase and confirm that you own it, and its library may send Google information about how the purchase went. All of this happens under Google's privacy policy, not ours. We never see your payment details. Droplet saves only whether the unlock is owned, on your device.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress, best scores and the unlock in its own private storage on your phone. We cannot read these files, and the game never sends them anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them.

Children

Droplet collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
